## For leadership

<Video name="pit-crew-for-leadership" title="Pit Crew for leadership" />

*5:15. Why it changes how a team works: AI that assists, agents that write code, every developer working their own way, and the proposal: one process for everyone, a plan agreed up front and every step verified with evidence. It closes on the open core: it runs with nothing connected, and each team plugs in its own tools.*
